About

From code to data platforms to AI.

I started as a developer who loved programming languages: Java, PHP, Python, Scala and C#. Over time, I moved to the harder problems below the code: messaging, storage, compute and distributed systems.

Big data became my field. I worked with Spark, Cassandra and Kafka, and later with Databricks on AWS. This path took me to data architecture, product building and leading teams.

Today I am Head of Data Engineering at Datamole in Brno. I set the technical direction, coach our architects and engineers, and lead our move into generative and agentic AI. I still write code.
