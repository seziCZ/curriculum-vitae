About

The engineering side of data.

I build data platforms: the systems that collect data, make it reliable and deliver it to products and AI agents. I do not build dashboards or train models.

I have a master's degree in computer science from Masaryk University, and I have built data systems since 2016. At Honeywell I worked with Spark, Kafka and Cassandra. At Atos I built an event-driven IoT platform on the actor model.

At Datamole I build data platforms on AWS and Databricks, and I have hired and grown two data engineering teams. Today I also lead our move into agentic AI. I use agents in my own work, so I know where they help and where they fail.

  • Ingest: Everything you build depends on data that arrives complete and on time.
  • Govern: People trust data only when its meaning, quality and access are clear.
  • Act: Apps, reports and AI agents turn this data into decisions and actions.

2024 to · Datamole

A data product for public transport

Transport operators needed reliable data to plan and run their services. I led our adoption of Databricks and built a team of 5 data engineers. We adopted Lakeflow early, so the team could focus on what the operators needed from their data. Today the platform processes the data behind about 500,000 public transport journeys a day in the Netherlands.

2018 to · Datamole

Predictive analytics for electron microscopes

Electron microscopes produce large amounts of data. I built one cloud-native platform for predictive analytics on this data, from 50+ AWS services defined fully in code with CDK. It now connects hundreds of microscopes. I also hired and grew the first team of 5.

2017 to 2018 · Atos

An IoT platform where milliseconds counted

I helped build one of the first full IoT platforms at Atos. The design was event-driven, with Akka and functional programming, for low latency, fault tolerance and scale.

Analytics for connected homes
